<h1>Eternal Tide</h1>
<p>Have you ever built a sandcastle?<br> Why did it never stand for long?<br> Because it was attacked, of course.
 </p>
<p>Now it’s your turn to protect it from everyone who tries to destroy it.
 </p>
<p>But be careful &mdash; under the blazing sun, even the strongest defender will grow tired if they never rest.
<br></p>
<h3>How to play?</h3>
<p><img src="https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzI3MzIyMzU3LnBuZw==/original/LeHph2.png"><br></p>
<p>Your goal is to defend the sandcastle. To do this, you must target  various enemies who will try to destroy it.&nbsp;The more you attack, the  smaller the damage zone becomes.&nbsp;
</p>
<p>As you progress through the  levels, the number of enemies increases, new types of enemies appear,  while the initial damage area decreases.&nbsp;</p>
